## 📌 Project Overview

This repository documents the business analysis and commercial strategy work completed during my internship with the **Newcastle Knights Commercial Team (The Wests Group)**. The project involved researching, analysing, and designing a **2-tier Business Club corporate membership program** to improve year-round corporate engagement, strengthen membership retention, and grow the Knights' commercial revenue base.

The deliverable was a stakeholder-ready business proposal covering market analysis, pricing strategy, target market segmentation, engagement model design, success measures, and a 3-year financial forecast — submitted to the commercial leadership team for strategic consideration.

> **Note:** Detailed financial data, proprietary commercial insights, and internal stakeholder materials have been appropriately redacted or summarised in this public repository to respect confidentiality obligations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The Newcastle Knights, like many NRL clubs, faced a common commercial challenge: corporate membership programs that were primarily event-driven rather than year-round, resulting in:

- Inconsistent corporate engagement outside the football season
- Limited product differentiation between corporate tiers
- Missed revenue opportunities from mid-market businesses seeking networking and brand association
- No structured engagement calendar to maintain relationships between game days

**Objective:** Design a commercially viable Business Club structure that delivers genuine value to corporate members year-round, supports sustainable revenue growth, and positions the Knights as a preferred corporate networking partner in the Hunter Valley and broader NSW market.

## 🔧 Methodology

### 1. Requirements Gathering & Stakeholder Engagement
- Conducted discussions with the commercial team to understand current program structure, commercial objectives, target market priorities, and constraints
- Reviewed existing membership data to identify engagement patterns and retention challenges
- Defined scope, success criteria, and deliverable expectations with commercial leadership

### 2. Competitor Benchmarking
- Researched and analysed **20+ business club and corporate membership models** across 11 NRL and AFL clubs
- Evaluated pricing structures, membership benefit packages, engagement calendars, naming conventions, and target market positioning
- Identified market gaps and best-practice approaches that could inform the Knights' program design

### 3. Market & Target Audience Analysis
- Segmented the corporate market by company size, engagement motivation, and budget capacity
- Identified two primary target segments corresponding to the proposed 2-tier structure:
  - **Tier 1 (Flagship):** Large corporates and key account partners seeking premium brand association, exclusive networking, and executive engagement
  - **Tier 2 (Associate):** SMEs and emerging businesses seeking affordable entry into the Knights' corporate community, networking access, and staff engagement

### 4. Program Design
- Designed a **2-tier Business Club structure** with distinct benefit packages, pricing, and engagement touchpoints for each tier
- Developed a **year-round engagement calendar** including networking events, game-day experiences, seasonal touchpoints, and exclusive member activities
- Mapped the proposed program against commercial objectives, sponsor alignment, and operational feasibility

### 5. Financial Modelling
- Built a **3-year financial forecast** using Excel, modelling membership uptake scenarios (conservative, base, optimistic)
- Analysed revenue projections, cost-to-serve estimates, and break-even analysis for each tier
- Incorporated pricing sensitivity analysis to stress-test revenue assumptions

### 6. Business Case & Recommendations
- Produced a stakeholder-ready proposal document including program overview, pricing rationale, engagement model, KPI framework, risk considerations, and implementation roadmap
- Presented findings and recommendations to the commercial leadership team
